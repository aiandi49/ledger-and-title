Before you reach out

Having a few basics ready makes the first conversation more useful:

  • What you're planning to buy, roughly, and when
  • Whether you already have an LLC formed, or are starting from nothing
  • The state you live in, and the state (if different) where the purchase will happen
  • Whether you're planning to pay cash or finance the purchase
