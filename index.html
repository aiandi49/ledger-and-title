WHY OWNERS DO THIS

An entity changes who signs the paperwork — and a few other things

Putting a purchase under an LLC moves the transaction onto a separate legal person's books. That single shift is behind most of the reasons people reach for it.

Liability separation

If the asset is owned by the LLC and something goes wrong with it, a claim generally reaches the LLC's assets first — not your house or personal savings, as long as the entity is run properly.

Public-record privacy

Titles, deeds, and registrations show the LLC's name, not yours. Anyone searching a plate or a property record finds the company, not your home address.

Cleaner tax paperwork

Business-use costs — fuel, depreciation, interest, maintenance — are easier to track and substantiate when every payment already runs through a dedicated business account.

A credit file of its own

Purchases and payments made through the LLC can build a business credit profile, which eventually lets the entity borrow without leaning on your personal score.
